## 📖 ABOUT THE UNIVERSE

**The SmokeJaguar Trilogy**
Eleanor Thorne's story begins in a cursed mansion in Gothic England — a bloodline bound to a hunger for power, and a woman who refuses to be her name. Across three games, she travels to the ends of the earth to break the curse for good:

1. **Eleanor: The Bloodline Curse** — Gothic England. Eleanor unmakes the Binding that has held her family for a hundred years.
2. **Eleanor: The Scales of Ma'at** — Egypt. Eleanor's heart is weighed against the feather of truth in the hall of the gods.
3. **Eleanor: The Shadow of the Jaguar** — the Aztec jungle. Eleanor confronts the source of the bloodline itself — and the jaguar that guards it.

**The Mansion Mysteries**
After the curse is broken, Eleanor and Neith make the mansion their home — and turn it into a base for solving the strange mysteries of Victorian England. A haunted mirror. A cursed family. A village with a bargain it will not speak of.

**The tone:**
Cozy-dark. Gothic mystery with a human heart. The dark is real, but it is always offset by the warmth between Eleanor and Neith — the light that makes the shadows worth facing.

## 👥 CHARACTERS

### Eleanor Thorne — the detective
The brilliant mind. A woman who spent her life refusing to be her bloodline's curse. Now she sees what others miss — and she will not look away from the truth, no matter what it costs her.

### Neith — the partner
A priestess of Ma'at who guarded the scales of judgement for a century. She sees what is hidden — in objects, in memories, in people. She is Eleanor's partner, and her light against the dark.

### Corvus — the collector *(antagonist)*
A distant Thorne who believes the family's power was stolen, not cursed. He is not evil — he is convinced. He wants to restore the name, and he will do terrible things to do it.

### The Beast of the moors
A black shape on the high ground, older than the village. Is it an escaped cat? A ghost? Or the guardian of an old bargain, waiting for someone who will refuse it?
