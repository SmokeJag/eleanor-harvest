# ELEANOR: CHILDREN OF THE HARVEST — Branch Map (draft)
> Three-pass plan to take the standalone from a single-session episode to a
> replayable multi-session game. Each pass is committed separately.

---

## WHERE WE ARE (the current shape)
- One investigation loop (the three old voices → the three truth-keys)
- Two ways to face the harvest (open the well / follow the child)
- Two climax resolutions (face the village / run with the child)
- One ending region (unmake the thing, sombre epilogue)

**That's ~1-2 sessions and little replay value.** We want hours + replay.

---

## PASS 1 — Deepen the investigation + the Beast of Exmoor thread
**Goal: real detective work, and a grounding-colour/tease thread.**

- **More investigation routes** beyond the three voices:
  - The chapel (the child is held there — a scene that raises stakes)
  - The records / schoolteacher's ledgers (a *written* layer of the history)
  - A second trip to the inn (the Beast is mentioned here first)
  - The moors (the Beast thread — a *tease*: colour for most, a thread for the curious)
- **Order/coverage matters:** some witnesses say more if you've talked to another first; clues can be *missed*.
- **The Beast of Exmoor** as grounding colour with a tease: villagers mutter about it; Eleanor dismisses it as escaped-pet folklore; a single, ambiguous sighting plants the seed. On the surface it's a red herring. (It pays off in Pass 3.)

**Deliverable:** the investigation becomes a web, not a line; the Beast threads into the setting.

---

## PASS 2 — Add the Neith trust/relationship axis
**Goal:** a second axis that changes how the story *plays*, for replay value.

- A `neith_trust` tracker (like the trilogy's `jaguar_trust`).
- Choices throughout build or erode it:
  - Whether you lean on her, trust her read on the village, hide your fear from her, defend her to the vicar, etc.
- **It changes the climax, not just flavour:**
  - High trust → she can help in a way a low-trust run cannot (a key intervention, a second pair of hands at the well).
  - Low trust → Eleanor is more alone at the moment it matters.
- A cold playthrough and a warm playthrough play noticeably differently.

**Deliverable:** a warm vs. distant axis with mechanical payoff.

---

## PASS 3 — Expand to a proper branch of endings
**Goal:** real outcomes, not flavour — plus a hidden path.

- **The Unmaking** (Eleanor ends the thing with the jaguar's words) — the "true" ending.
- **The Refusal** (the village turns on the thing) — a costlier, more communal ending.
- **The Beast path (hidden):** only reachable if you investigated the moors *and* trusted Neith enough. The Beast is revealed as the *other* side of the bargain — a guardian, or the thing's opposite. Leads to a third, secret ending.
- **Different fates** for the child, Maren, the village, and the Beast depending on choices.

**Deliverable:** a proper branch of outcomes — one playthrough cannot see the whole game.

---

## REPLAY MAP (at a glance)
| Choice | Path | What it changes |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Who to investigate (and when) | The web | What clues you find / miss |
| Lean on Neith vs. go alone | The axis | How she can help at the climax |
| Open the well vs. find the child | The approach | Which confrontations you see |
| Explore the moors + trust her | The hidden path | Unlocks the Beast ending |
| Unseal vs. refuse vs. Beast | The endings | Fates of the child, village, Beast |
